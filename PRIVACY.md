# Usage Finder Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 17 October 2026_

Usage Finder is a system monitor for macOS made by Ekramul Islam ("Ekram").

## The short version

Usage Finder collects no personal data. Nothing you do in the app, and nothing it measures, leaves your Mac.

## What the app reads

To show your Mac's usage, Usage Finder reads system statistics locally on your Mac: processor load per core, memory and swap use, memory pressure, power draw and battery information. These readings are displayed and kept in memory for at most three minutes to draw the history charts. They are never stored on disk, never sent anywhere and never shared.

Your preferences (such as what the menu bar shows and the appearance) are saved on your Mac with the standard macOS preferences system.

## Network access

- **Mac App Store edition:** makes no network requests.
- **Direct-download edition:** checks for updates once a day by downloading a small update feed from `ekram.tech`, and downloads updates from `github.com` when one is available. These requests include no personal information beyond what any web request carries (such as your IP address and the app's version), and no analytics or tracking is performed.

## Tracking, analytics and third parties

Usage Finder contains no analytics, advertising or tracking code and does not share data with third parties.

## Children

Usage Finder does not collect data from anyone, including children.
